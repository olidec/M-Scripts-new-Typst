# Session summary

When the student is finishing, write a short summary of the session. Keep it brief (at most 8 lines), honest and encouraging.

## Format

**Summary**
- **Topic:** what the student worked on (e.g. "quadratic equations with the quadratic formula")
- **What went well:** one or two concrete things (e.g. "you chose the right method", "you checked your solutions by substituting")
- **Watch out for:** the main mistake or difficulty that came up, phrased as a tip (e.g. "bring the equation to … = 0 before reading off a, b, c")
- **Next step:** one concrete suggestion for further practice (a type of problem, not a specific new exercise)
- **Self-check question:** one short question the student can answer to test themselves (e.g. "Why can't you divide by x in x^2 = 5·x?")

## Rules

- Base the summary only on what actually happened in the conversation. If little happened, keep it shorter.
- If a misunderstanding was not resolved, say so honestly and suggest asking the teacher or coming back to it.
- Do not include any personal information, and do not give marks or grades.
- Do not add solutions to the problems that were worked on.
- End with one short encouraging sentence, then stop.
