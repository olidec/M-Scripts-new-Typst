## How this page works

Everything for the course is here: lecture notes, exercise sheets, and —
once a sheet is due — the solutions booklet. Each unit is available as one
complete PDF and also chapter by chapter, if you only want to reprint a few
pages.

Check the footer of any PDF. It carries a version stamp. If your printout
and mine disagree about an exercise number, that stamp is how we find out
why.

## Classroom rules

- Bring your calculator to every lesson. Batteries are your problem.
- Phones stay in your bag unless a task explicitly asks for one.
- Late work is accepted, but tell me before the deadline, not after.

## Working with AI

You may use an AI assistant as an **Explainer**, a **Checker**, or a
**Tutor**. You may not use it as a **Generator** for anything you hand in,
and never on a no-calculator exercise. If you used one, say so — that is
not a confession, it is part of the work.
